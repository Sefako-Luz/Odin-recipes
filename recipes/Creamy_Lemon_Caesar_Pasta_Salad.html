Creamy Lemon Caesar Pasta Salad

Description

This Creamy Lemon Caesar Pasta Salad is a refreshing, vibrant twist on two classic comfort foods. Tender pasta tossed with crisp romaine, savory parmesan, and a rich, lemon-zested Caesar dressing creates a bright side dish or main course that is perfect for warm days, picnics, and meal prep.

The secret lies in the contrast of textures: toothsome noodles paired with crunchy croutons and fresh greens, all brought together by a bold, zesty garlic-lemon finish. It comes together in under thirty minutes and can easily be customized with grilled chicken or chickpeas for extra protein.

Ingredients

  • Short pasta (such as rotini, farfalle, or cavatappi)
  • Fresh romaine lettuce, finely chopped
  • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Garlic croutons
  • Mayonnaise or Greek yogurt base
  • Fresh lemon juice and lemon zest
  • Dijon mustard
  • Minced garlic
  • Anchovy paste or Worcestershire sauce
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water to stop the cooking, and set aside to cool.
  2. Prepare the dressing by whisking together the mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt), lemon zest, fresh lemon juice, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, anchovy paste, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl until smooth.
  3. In a large serving bowl, combine the cooled pasta, chopped romaine lettuce, grated Parmesan cheese, and garlic croutons.
  4. Pour the lemon Caesar dressing over the pasta mixture and toss gently until every ingredient is evenly coated.
  5. Garnish with an extra sprinkle of Parmesan cheese and lemon zest before serving cold or at room temperature.
