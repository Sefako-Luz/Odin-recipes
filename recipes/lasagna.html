Lasagna

Description

Nothing satisfies a crowd quite like a pan of rich, bubbling home-baked lasagna. Featuring alternating layers of hearty meat sauce, velvety ricotta cheese mixture, tender pasta sheets, and melted mozzarella, this timeless Italian dish delivers comfort and depth of flavor in every layer.

Slow-simmered tomato sauce spiked with garlic, Italian herbs, and savory ground beef or sausage guarantees a robust foundation. Prepared a day in advance or baked fresh, it slices cleanly into generous portions that taste even better as leftover comfort food

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or Italian sausage (or a mix of both)
  • Diced yellow onion and minced garlic
  • Crushed tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • Egg (to bind the cheese layer)
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Italian seasoning (basil, oregano, thyme)
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat, brown the ground meat with the onion and garlic until fully cooked, then drain any excess fat.
  2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer the sauce on low heat for 20 to 30 minutes to let the flavors meld.
  3. Boil the lasagna noodles in salted water until flexible, then lay them flat on parchment paper to prevent sticking (or use oven-ready noodles).
  4. In a medium bowl, mix together the ricotta cheese, egg, half of the chopped parsley, half of the Parmesan, salt, and pepper until uniform.
  5. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  6. Assemble the layers: place a single layer of noodles, spread a third of the ricotta mixture, top with meat sauce, and sprinkle with mozzarella. Repeat these layers twice more.
  7. Top the final layer of noodles with the remaining sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese. Cover loosely with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 15 to 20 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling. Let rest 15 minutes before slicing.
