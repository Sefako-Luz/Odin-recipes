Skillet Peach Cornbread

Description

Skillet Peach Cornbread is a sweet and savory Southern classic elevated by juicy, fresh summer peaches. Baked directly in a heavy cast-iron skillet, this recipe produces a crispy, buttery golden crust on the outside with a tender, moist crumb on the inside.

As the peaches bake down into the cornmeal batter, their sweet juices create jammy pockets of flavor throughout the bread. Served warm straight from the pan with a drizzle of honey or a scoop of vanilla ice cream, it makes an unforgettable side dish or casual dessert.

Ingredients

  • Yellow cornmeal
  • All-purpose flour
  • Granulated sugar or honey
  • Baking powder and baking soda
  • Salt
  • Buttermilk (or whole milk mixed with a splash of lemon juice)
  • Unsalted butter, melted (plus extra for greasing the skillet)
  • Large eggs
  • Fresh or frozen peach slices
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon or nutmeg (optional)

Steps

  1. Place a 10-inch cast-iron skillet inside your oven and preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) so the skillet gets piping hot.
  2. Whisk together the dry ingredients in a large bowl: yellow cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
  3. In a separate bowl, combine the wet ingredients: buttermilk, melted butter, and eggs until smooth.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined (be careful not to overmix).
  5. Carefully remove the hot cast-iron skillet from the oven, add two tablespoons of butter, and swirl it around until melted and coating the bottom and sides.
  6. Pour the batter into the hot skillet, then arrange the peach slices neatly over the top of the batter in a fan pattern.
  7. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the edges are deep golden brown. Cool slightly before slicing into wedges.
